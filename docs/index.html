Data comes from EA's public Pro Clubs match records for league and playoff games. EA only keeps each club's most recent matches, and often publishes them hours late, so a scheduled collector saves new games here every hour from 10 PM to 3 AM Pakistan time each night, at 9 AM and 12 PM Monday to Thursday to catch late games, and every hour from 10 AM to 9 PM on Fridays to Sundays (always 19 minutes past). It also saves each opponent's club record and their players' lines, and retrains the prediction model whenever new games arrive, then rebuilds the Review tab, which replays every session with the model trained only on earlier games and checks its forecasts against what happened.This copy is a static snapshot of that data; the live version updates itself as games are saved. Games played before collection started can't be recovered; the Overview tab lists any that were missed. Team shot, pass and tackle figures cover human players only; goals include AI teammates. Impact score is this dashboard's own blend of EA rating, goal involvement, passing, defending and results, shown per player under "How the score is built". Minutes and idle seconds are EA's real-time counters for each player. EA's public feed has no pitch positions, heatmaps, minute-by-minute ratings or time on the ball, so those can't be shown. Match actions (interceptions, possession, dribbles, positioning prompts and decision feedback) come from EA's event counters, decoded by the community research at github.com/Interactive-63/eafc-pro-clubs-api-research; EA has no passes-received counter.
